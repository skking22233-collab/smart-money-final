Precious Metals Analysis: Evaluating Gold and Silver Volatility in 2026

A
Arif Khan, Lead Financial Analyst January 14, 2026 Gold & Silver

Precious metals have had one of the biggest swings in recent memory. Gold went above $5,000 per ounce this week but then fell sharply. Gold now trades around $4,800 to $4,900 per ounce because the dollar is strong and investors are moving to safety. Silver has been more dramatic - falling as much as 17 to 36 percent in a single day, dropping to about $72 to $73 per ounce, later rising to $75 to $77 per ounce, wiping out most of the gains made in early 2026.

What's Causing the Selloff?

  • Profit-taking after surges (gold doubled in the year)
  • Position liquidations
  • Spillover from crypto and tech weakness
  • Silver being more vulnerable as both industrial metal (solar, EVs) and investment asset

Expert Predictions

JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs still see upside for gold - could reach $5,400 to $6,300 per ounce by year end or higher because of:

  • Central bank buying
  • Inflation hedges
  • Geopolitical risks (U.S.-Iran tensions)
  • De-dollarization trends

UBS says recent dips are just volatility in a structural uptrend.

Global Demand

ETFs and physical demand remain robust. Central banks continue accumulating. Investors rotate into metals as safe haven amid equity uncertainty. In Asia (India, China) and Europe, retail buying on dips has supported price floors.

Silver: The Affordable Alternative

Silver is way cheaper than gold at just Rs. 4,200 per tola - less than 2% of gold's cost. Plus, it's giving 15% returns in six months.

Why Silver is Hot:

  • Industries need it for solar panels, gadgets, and electric cars
  • Green energy boom - Pakistan's solar adoption rising due to power problems
  • Chinese factories using tons of silver for solar cells
  • Demand in 2025 exceeded mine production
  • U.S. rules on Russian metals and Red Sea shipping issues increasing demand

Silver Trend in Pakistan

  • Traders in Hyderabad report business way up
  • Investors melting old stuff to make silver bars
  • Women in Sukkur forming groups to save in silver (can't afford gold)
  • Stores like Gul Ahmed now selling silver alongside jewelry

One mom, Fatima, said: "I bought gold for my daughter's wedding, but silver is for her education later on."

Pakistan's Unique Position

  • Rare earth deals in 2026 and closer ties with China
  • Gold smuggling issues - silver doesn't get stolen as much
  • IMF limiting gold imports through duties, pushing people to silver
  • IT exports growing, needing more chips containing silver
  • Climate issues (floods, heatwaves) driving solar adoption requiring silver

How to Invest

  • Beginners: Get silver coins from dealers like Pakistan Mint
  • Experienced: Use PMEX futures for silver (watch for rupee risks)
  • Tax: You'll need to pay taxes on gains after a year

Success Story: A Lahore trader, Imran, bought silver worth Rs. 1 lakh and turned it into Rs. 1.6 lakh in six months. Gold would've only made half that!

Outlook

Expect choppiness short-term, but structural drivers (debt concerns, trade wars) favor higher prices. Many experts advise 5-10% portfolio allocation for diversification.
